# Case-study card — Policy Lab / CL-ECI

## Thirty-second account

Policy Lab is open research software for making evidence-backed financial and public-policy decisions inspectable. It separates evidence assurance, policy admission, quantity limits, settlement consequences and decision lineage so a reviewer can see both the decision and the basis for it.

- repository: `Spectating101/solarpunk-coin`;
- deployment: public static research workbench;
- license: MIT for Policy Lab code, with third-party materials retaining their own terms;
- local CI repair: `03e331c` on `fix/policy-live-smoke-bootstrap-20260917`, not canonical until promoted and green.

## Demonstrated work

- live public surface returns the correct Policy Lab identity;
- public-data checkpoint remains outside the controlled case pack;
- rendered decision is `ADMIT WITH LIMIT`, not an unqualified approval;
- four cases, three policies and four assurance scenarios are exposed;
- evidence exists across all nine Digital Public Goods indicators;
- documentation covers architecture, schemas, policies, reproduction, contribution, governance, privacy and security;
- import/export uses ordinary formats including CSV, JSON and Markdown;
- tests and schemas preserve deterministic evidence/decision lineage.

## Why it matters

The strongest story is not that Policy Lab has received an external standards verdict. It is that the system can turn heterogeneous evidence into a constrained, reviewable decision while keeping ownership, uncertainty, privacy and legal authority visible.

## Strongest nonclaims

- Policy Lab is not a recognised Digital Public Good;
- no government, university, company or operator adoption is evidenced;
- no regulator has endorsed its thresholds or decisions;
- no measured institutional, financial-inclusion or policy impact is claimed;
- the static privacy architecture does not prove compliance with every jurisdiction.

## Best Main Quest uses

- financial AI, FinTech and empirical-policy employment packet;
- inspectable policy/assurance case study;
- funded research on evidence-bounded decision systems;
- supporting evidence for public-interest and responsible-AI work.

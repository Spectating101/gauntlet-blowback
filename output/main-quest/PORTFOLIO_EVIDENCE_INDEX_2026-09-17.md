## Selection rules

1. A packet leads with one or two assets that directly answer the audience's problem; it does not dump the entire portfolio.
2. Supporting systems supply method or implementation evidence only when the connection is explicit.
3. Exact test counts, deployment states and commit identifiers must be re-read from their canonical authority before release.
4. Internal tests establish reproducibility and engineering maturity, not external usefulness or adoption.
5. Every case study carries its strongest nonclaim beside its strongest proof.

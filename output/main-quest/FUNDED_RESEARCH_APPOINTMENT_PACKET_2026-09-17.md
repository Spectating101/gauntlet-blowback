# Funded research appointment packet — internal master

## Research identity

Christopher Ongko works at the intersection of empirical finance, reliable research software and evidence-gated agent systems. The unifying question is practical: how can a system use models to accelerate research or engineering while keeping sources, uncertainty and consequential decisions inspectable?

This text is a reusable internal source. It is not addressed to a host, does not assert a collaboration and is not a submitted proposal.

## Research programme A — reliable research workflows

**Question:** Can an AI-assisted research workflow preserve the exact source path from ingestion to grounded synthesis and final export under restart and failure?  
**Current base:** Cite-Agent and Research Drive.  
**Internal demonstration:** fresh question/input; preserved source bytes and locators; grounded result; empirical or synthesis step; manuscript/export; restart; invalidation; forced worker failure; no duplicate or false completion.  
**Potential outputs:** evaluated workflow, failure taxonomy, reproducibility package and systems paper.  
**Boundary:** external researcher utility remains unproven until real independent use occurs.

## Research programme B — evidence-gated agent systems

**Question:** How should an agentic system separate recommendation, evidence and authority when software outputs could trigger physical or institutional action?  
**Current base:** Hardware Splicer, Refinery and Public-Good Control Plane.  
**Internal demonstration:** matched non-destructive evaluation; parity audit; capability/provider resolution; stale-mapping refusal; auditable human decision and correction loop.  
**Potential outputs:** evaluation protocol, benchmark fixtures, reusable authority/receipt model and systems case study.  
**Boundary:** no physical correctness, field effectiveness or deployment outcome is claimed.

## Research programme C — inspectable empirical policy systems

**Question:** Can empirical claims, assurance decisions and policy consequences be represented so a reviewer can trace the evidence and see what remains unknown?  
**Current base:** Policy Lab / CL-ECI, Fiscal Choke Points and later Invisible Ledger.  
**Internal demonstration:** new-case evidence intake; assurance decision; policy comparison; quantitative consequence/export; tampered and insufficient-evidence negatives; reproducible paper package.  
**Potential outputs:** working paper, policy-system evaluation and transparent case corpus.  
**Boundary:** no policy adoption, regulator endorsement or causal effect is implied.

## Methods and working style

- reproducible empirical analysis with frozen inputs and explicit assumptions;
- source/provenance preservation from ingestion to output;
- negative and failure-path testing, not only happy-path demos;
- exact claim/nonclaim ledgers;
- human gates for legal, ethical, financial and consequential commitments;
- durable receipts and restartable workflows.

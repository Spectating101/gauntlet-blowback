## First-pilot protocol

Use a **closed/public information-manipulation or evolving-claim case** with lawful public-source evidence.

Measure at minimum:

- time to reconstruct the current documented state;
- time to locate supporting sources;
- visibility of corrections and supersession;
- duplicate checking avoided;
- analyst confidence / error notes.

The comparison should be against the partner's ordinary process where feasible. The purpose is to measure workflow delta, not to ask the partner to endorse marketing language.

Do not manufacture pilot value through private-person surveillance, harassment, punitive identity dossiers or other targeting behavior.

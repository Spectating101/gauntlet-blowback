## Automation Boundary

Near-zero marginal application cost is a valid operating objective, but only after eligibility, evidence, duplication, and reputational constraints are enforced. The operator should autonomously research, map portals, resolve facts from canonical evidence, draft and compress supplied copy, upload designated artifacts, and save drafts. It should not autonomously invent eligibility, accept legal or privacy terms, make originality or authorship declarations, pay fees, bypass anti-bot controls, or execute final Submit without valid route-specific authority.

This boundary still allows almost all application labor to be automated. The remaining human action should normally be one small protected commitment at the end, not repeated navigation or form-entry work.

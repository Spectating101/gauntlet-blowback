## Assessment

Gauntlet plus the real Chrome session can perform most of the mechanical preparation for an eligible application: read instructions, map current fields, fill canonical facts, adapt supplied copy to field limits, upload designated artifacts, save a draft and track the resulting receipt. It can operate an unfamiliar portal through the generic mission interface; a portal-specific script or FIRE manifest is not required to start safe work.

The current list is not a queue of 187 ready applications. Some records are jobs, source families, watches, events, expired calls, research still needing results or routes with outside prerequisites. Final submission, consent, personal declarations, CAPTCHA, payments and adviser/team commitments remain protected by AGENTS.md. These gates persist under the user's general automation request.

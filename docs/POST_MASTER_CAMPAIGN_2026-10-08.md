## Taiwan work-rights wording

The [Workforce Development Agency's official guidance](https://ezworktaiwan.wda.gov.tw/en/cp.aspx?n=8CE17C2739F9AB35&s=9BEFF2CF37C2786B) says Taiwan degree graduates with NIA-approved extended residence may work without a separate work permit during that residence, for up to two years.

## Proposed research direction

I want to study how AI can help a researcher understand the approaches that already exist for a problem. Refinery is the project through which I would pursue this. The starting point is the problem itself: compare what different systems do, examine the sources behind those descriptions, and identify important differences or gaps.

A first study would focus on tools for reconciling research datasets. I would evaluate whether a system that reads and compares their documentation gives a more useful account than repository search alone. Software reuse would be a later application of the research, rather than its sole purpose.

# Post-Graduation Conversion Board

Snapshot: 2026-08-26.

This is the post-semester counterpart to the long-tail Blowback radar. The long-tail lane seeks frequent smaller external conversion during the final semester; this lane seeks the strongest external state to enter after graduation.

## Objective

Do not choose `JOB` or `PHD` as an identity in advance.

Use the active portfolio as the asset base, fire low-cost applications upward into both markets, collect external pricing, and make concrete offers compete.

Primary evidence clusters:

1. **Agent / research systems** — Cite-Agent, YZU / Research Drive, Citation Engine, Blowback.
2. **Quant / finance / empirical research** — Finance MSc, Sharpe Terminus, thesis/papers, large-data RA work.
3. **Industrial AI / computer vision** — Hardware Splicer.
4. **Trust / governance / public-interest research** — Policy Lab, Nocturnal, Public-Good Control, Citation Engine.

## Application doctrine

Applications should be cheap; acceptance should be hard.

- A high-fit job with undisclosed compensation is `PRICE_DISCOVERY`, not an automatic reject.
- A PhD with good funding but unknown founder/IP rules is `FIRE_DUE_DILIGENCE`, not an automatic recommendation.
- Median salary is a market-floor reference, not the target valuation for an upper-tail campaign.
- The portfolio must be tested against roles where unusual evidence can change the hiring bucket.
- Never count hypothetical startup upside as realized value.
- Never count university project funding as personal income.
- Never count employer equity/bonus that is not in an actual offer.

## Economic search policy

Configured initial search thresholds are deliberately aggressive and are hypotheses to test against the market:

- `job_backstop_floor_annual_ntd`: NT$1.2M
- `job_serious_floor_annual_ntd`: NT$1.5M
- `job_high_value_floor_annual_ntd`: NT$2.0M
- `phd_min_monthly_funding_ntd`: NT$30k

These are campaign-routing thresholds, not claims about personal entitlement or guaranteed offers.

A job below the serious-search floor can still survive if it has unusually strong equity, autonomy, work-life balance, visa, or commercial-option value. A PhD below the funding floor can survive only if its non-cash economics are exceptionally strong and verified.

## Empirical comparison fields

For jobs:

- published/inferred total compensation range and confidence;
- base / bonus / equity when eventually known;
- expected controlled hours and commute;
- remote/hybrid;
- role/portfolio fit;
- degree/seniority/language blockers;
- employer signal and future compensation re-rating;
- moonlighting / IP restrictions;
- exit flexibility.

For PhDs:

- salary/stipend and employee-vs-scholarship status;
- tuition/fees;
- teaching/admin load;
- research scope freedom;
- outside paid work;
- founder/director/share ownership;
- compensated work in own company;
- pre-existing IP protection;
- ownership of improvements/new research IP;
- commercialization/spin-off/incubator infrastructure;
- grants, compute, industry/customer access;
- duration and exit cost.

Unknowns stay `UNKNOWN`; they are not scored as positive merely because the institution has an entrepreneurship webpage.

## Sorting rule

`FIRE_NOW`

- hard deadline <= 30 days and plausible fit, or
- rolling high-value job with low application cost and no known hard blocker.

`FIRE_SOON`

- deadline 31–90 days;
- Taiwan admissions call expected but not yet published;
- requires one reusable packet not yet ready.

`PRICE_DISCOVERY`

- fit is good and application is cheap, but compensation is undisclosed.

`WATCH`

- employer/program family is strategically useful but no currently verified requisition/call is ready.

`BACKSTOP`

- economics are below current search floor or strategic upside is weak.

`KILL`

- hard eligibility/language/degree blocker, expired deadline, or new-build/application burden is irrational relative to upside.

## Calendar semantics

Hard deadlines live in `calendar/gauntlet-postgrad-2026-2027.ics`.

Rolling jobs do **not** receive fake closing dates. The calendar instead contains recurring campaign blocks:

- Monday: refresh upper-tail job boards and expire stale roles.
- Tuesday: fire quant/finance batch.
- Thursday: fire AI/research-engineering batch.
- Friday: update screens/interviews/offers and economic evidence.
- twice monthly: refresh Taiwan PhD calls and commercial/IP due-diligence.

## Decision point

The final decision occurs at concrete-offer stage, not at category stage.

A job and a PhD are compared only after replacing assumptions with offer-specific facts. The purpose of this board is to create enough competing external options that graduation becomes a selection problem rather than a cliff.

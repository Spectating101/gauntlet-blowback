## Operating rule

Treat **Fiscal Choke Points (DT)** and **Invisible Ledger (IL)** as separate conversion-capable research assets.

- **Fiscal Choke Points / DT** owns tax administration, comparative taxation, regulatory intermediation, comparative public policy, fiscal capacity, and digital-economy tax routes.
- **Invisible Ledger / IL** owns digital-economy measurement, platform economy, e-commerce measurement, accounting/disclosure, official statistics, payment traces, and administrative-visibility routes.
- **Paired DT + IL** is an umbrella research program only where the external call genuinely values both economic observability and public administrative placement. Do not merge the papers or reuse one paper as evidence for the other.

The combined program can be expressed as:

`economic activity → private/statistical/payment records → administrative visibility → fiscal placement → procedural capacity`

That sequence is a research-program map, not a claim that the two papers jointly identify one causal effect.

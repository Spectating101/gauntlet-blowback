## Automatic stacking queue

After every eligible external academic competition:

1. preserve registration confirmation;
2. preserve official rules / category classification;
3. preserve final submitted artifact;
4. preserve result/finalist/winner receipt;
5. preserve eligible expense receipts;
6. trigger YZU competition-subsidy eligibility check while student status remains valid;
7. record reimbursement as `cost_displaced_ntd`, not competition-prize cash.

## InnoServe optimization requirement

Before any InnoServe submission, solve the parent-program constraint globally:

- verify current enrollment eligibility;
- enumerate all project × track candidates;
- verify per-team/per-project/category caps;
- remove tracks that require meaningful new build;
- score remaining candidates by smallest cash-bearing slot, slot density, fit, prep time and strategic validation;
- select the valid subset rather than submitting opportunistically one-by-one.

## Weekly operating cadence

### Monday — discover / verify
- ingest new Taiwan competition and university-board records;
- ingest current OSS bounty/microgrant issues;
- ingest public-benefit/research mini-grants;
- ingest north-Taiwan/remote research-engineering roles;
- expire stale records and snapshot changed rules.

### Tuesday — eligibility / packet
- resolve machine-verifiable rules;
- surface only genuinely necessary user facts;
- generate/adapt dossiers for top READY candidates;
- check duplicate/category constraints.

### Wednesday–Thursday — FIRE
- human review of consequential submissions;
- submit / send / claim issue;
- preserve confirmation/receipt immediately.

### Friday — outcome / economics
- follow up pending campaigns;
- record selections/rejections/meetings/cash/credits;
- compute trailing 30-day realized conversion;
- compute conversion per marginal human hour;
- downweight sources with poor verified hit rate.

## First empirical benchmark

Do not judge Blowback by the largest award won.

Judge the first campaign batch by:

- 30-day verified FIRE count;
- median prep minutes;
- cash-bearing opportunities per source;
- actual cash realized;
- actual AI/tool cost displaced;
- number of external selections/meetings/pilots;
- source and asset hit rates;
- new-build hours caused by opportunities.

Success condition for the first operating phase:

> Demonstrate whether NT$5,000 trailing-30-day cash + genuine AI/tool cost displacement can be sustained without restarting portfolio construction.

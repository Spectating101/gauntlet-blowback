## Operating rule

Treat the **contribution lane**, not merely the repository, as the unit of external conversion:

- **Invisible Ledger (IL)** — empirical platform-economy / digital-tax / finance paper.
- **CL** — general constrained-authorization / evidence-to-authority architecture. Use alone only where architectural generality is the native fit.
- **ECI** — energy-constrained finance / sustainable-finance mechanism. Use alone where energy/green-finance specificity improves fit.
- **CL-ECI / Policy Lab** — integrated executable research system separating evidence assurance, policy admission, supported quantity, settlement, and lineage.

Do not create duplicate-publication spam. A poster, fellowship, grant, standards review, product competition, and paper can coexist when they are genuinely different conversion channels. Substantially overlapping peer-reviewed/proceedings manuscripts require an explicit overlap check before firing.

## Asset-specific conversion doctrine

### Invisible Ledger

The current paper is strong enough to run a dedicated external-validation lane. The safe core claim is the audited Indonesia-focused platform estimate and the bounded reporting/tax-administration interpretation; the ASEAN figure remains a calibration scenario.

Preferred conversions:

1. tax-policy / tax-administration research funding;
2. finance/FinTech paper presentation;
3. selective international-tax workshops/symposia;
4. productized reporting-observability competition entry only when the venue explicitly wants a service/system rather than a conventional paper.

Do **not** reopen the empirical manuscript merely to fit a generic AI call.

### ECI

Lead with ECI when an energy/sustainable-finance venue rewards domain specificity. GRASFI-Asia is the current strongest example because its CFP explicitly includes Energy Finance, sustainability-linked products, sustainability metrics/reporting, and sustainable asset pricing.

Do not force ECI into generic software/open-source calls when CL-ECI carries the stronger architecture.

### CL

CL is primarily a packaging lens unless a call explicitly values protocol architecture, policy-as-code, authorization/trust, auditability, or general-purpose evidence-to-authority boundaries. Keep the energy case as a demonstration rather than deleting it unless the call benefits from abstraction.

### CL-ECI / Policy Lab

Keep the existing primary manuscript lane at FC'27. Global AI Finance is a WIP/poster channel, SSI is a fellowship channel, DPG is a standards-review channel, NLnet is a funding channel, and product competitions are application/demo channels. Those can run in parallel subject to truthful claim boundaries.

## Overlap guard

Before any `READY_TO_PREPARE` promotion, record:

- contribution statement;
- artifact submitted (poster / abstract / full paper / proposal / demo packet / grant application);
- publication/proceedings status;
- exclusivity/originality rule;
- overlap with active submissions;
- whether a new result or merely new packaging is required.

If the only way to create a second paper submission is to rename the same contribution, set `DUPLICATE_OR_CATEGORY_LIMIT` / `G5` and HOLD or KILL.

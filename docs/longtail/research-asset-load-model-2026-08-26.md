## Saturation rule

At any time, permit at most:

- **1 deep manuscript campaign** (>5 h substantive writing/research),
- **2 medium packaging campaigns** (2–5 h), and
- any number of **cheap verification/portal campaigns** (<1 h) that do not interrupt deep work.

If a new route would exceed this, rank it by expected external signal × native fit × reuse / marginal work and defer or KILL the weakest route.

Through 17 Sep the protected deep manuscript slot is **FC'27 / CL-ECI**. GRASFI may take the slot only if standalone ECI is already essentially submission-ready; it must not steal several days of new research from FC merely because its deadline is earlier.

## Expected conversion portfolio

The tranche intentionally spreads risk across different external verdict mechanisms:

| Conversion type | Route examples | What a positive verdict proves |
|---|---|---|
| research presentation | GRASFI, Shih Hsin, FTSID | external academic interest / presentation acceptance |
| peer-reviewed proceedings | FC'27 | manuscript-level technical review |
| research funding | Tax Academy, NLnet | external willingness to fund next work under stated scope |

| product competition | Chunghwa, NTUB | application/demo legibility and competitive product evaluation |
| standards/public-interest review | DPG | standards-based public-interest/reusability validation |
| selective tax symposium | Global Tax Symposium | high-signal scholarly discussion invitation |

Do not convert one type of verdict into another. A competition finalist result does not prove empirical correctness; a paper acceptance does not prove market adoption; a DPG listing does not prove monetary performance.

- CL-ECI competes in technical FinTech/research-software routes;

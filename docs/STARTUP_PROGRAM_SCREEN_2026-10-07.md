## Claude-specific terms and next step

The [additional-benefits addendum](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/startup-program-addendum) is effective October 6. Third-party stack offers have their own conditions, are generally one-time/non-transferable, and have no cash value. It prohibits automated access/harvesting of Stack Benefits. This public eligibility screen did not access member offers. Human review is required before accepting terms or redeeming benefits. [Program terms](https://www.anthropic.com/startup-program-official-terms) also require authority to bind the applicant and leave selection discretionary.

Prepare one factual applicant/company profile: legal/trading identity, actual founding date, ownership of the selected product, public site/domain email, incorporation if any, funding, existing program credits and current Team subscription. Then use the [official Console application](https://platform.claude.com/offers/startups-application) and inspect the live requirements. Preserve no-user/no-revenue boundaries where applicable. Choose the narrative from actual workload and product evidence; the nine-project deck is supporting evidence, not nine fictitious startup applications.

The new Claude, DigitalOcean and Cloudflare leads are recorded as held eligibility-recon routes; existing AWS/Microsoft/Google gates are refreshed. All are undated leads: no invented calendar deadline or executable FIRE status.

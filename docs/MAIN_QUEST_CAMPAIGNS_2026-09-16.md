# Main Quest Campaign Architecture — 2026-09-16

Gauntlet should not behave like a calendar with hundreds of unrelated entries. The calendar is an execution surface. The strategic unit is a **campaign**.

The governing rule is:

> **Discover broadly. Organize by desired outcome. Execute through campaigns.**

An opportunity is an instrument inside a plan, not the plan itself. Priority controls sequence, not census inclusion.

## Terminal campaign A — Funded PhD option creation

Objective: create multiple genuinely funded doctoral options, then compare real admissions and funding packages rather than pre-committing to one institution.

```text
research + systems portfolio
        ↓
research identity
        ↓
61-route Taiwan PhD census
        ↓
program + faculty mapping
        ↓
funding / scholarship mapping
        ↓
reusable master dossier
        ↓
route-specific deltas
        ↓
applications
        ↓
interviews / faculty contact
        ↓
funded offers
        ↓
offer comparison
```

Every PhD route should eventually carry: formal eligibility, selection mechanics, seats where available, faculty fit, funding amount/certainty/duration, tuition/fees, recommenders, documents, deadlines, lead evidence family, route-specific delta and competition state.

The success criterion is **option creation**, not admission to one preselected school.

## Terminal campaign B — Employment option creation

Objective: create serious offers across distinct labor-market families and learn what the market is willing to pay for the combined finance, research and AI-systems profile.

```text
portfolio
   ↓
88-route employment census
   ↓
role-family mapping
   ↓
formal gates + economics
   ↓
reusable packet families
   ↓
route-specific deltas
   ↓
applications
   ↓
interviews
   ↓
offers
   ↓
compensation + career comparison
```

Five working packet families:

1. **Research / applied AI** — Refinery + Cite/YZUC + Research Drive + Hardware Splicer.
2. **Financial AI / FinTech** — Finance MSc + settled empirical research + applied AI systems. Unresolved thesis material is not a default employment claim.
3. **Quant** — Finance MSc + Sharpe/Terminus + empirical work + Python/data engineering.
4. **AI/software/agent systems** — Refinery + Cite + YZUC/Research Drive.
5. **Semiconductor / EDA / physical AI** — Hardware Splicer + Refinery.

Every employment route should eventually carry: live requisition state, formal gates, compensation, work authorization, location, lead evidence family, packet family, marginal application cost and competition state.

## Support campaign — Academic Evidence Factory

Academic work should manufacture stronger Main Quest evidence instead of becoming a conference-activity treadmill.

Working order:

**Fiscal Choke Points / DT → Invisible Ledger → CEIR / Energy Anchoring → ESG Rebrand**, unless an unusually valuable external opportunity justifies a temporary reorder.

- **DT:** external validation → journal-quality publication. Strengthens PhD, policy/public-administration and financial-AI credibility.
- **Invisible Ledger:** thesis authority → flagship paper. Strengthens empirical/finance/quant/research credibility. Current hard gate is sample/perimeter authority before downstream venue activity.
- **CEIR:** empirical upgrade → publication candidate. Strengthens finance/quant evidence.
- **ESG Rebrand:** data + identification completion → working paper. Strengthens empirical-methods evidence.

## Support campaign — Systems Evidence Factory

The systems portfolio should be converted into inspectable evidence that can be reused across both Main Quest lanes.

```text
working system
    ↓
reproducible evaluation
    ↓
real-user / independent evidence
    ↓
canonical case study
    ↓
research/software credibility
    ↓
PhD + employment packets
```

- **Refinery:** general AI systems / reliability / research-software identity.
- **Hardware Splicer:** physical/EDA/agentic-AI proof.
- **Cite/YZUC/Research Drive:** research tooling, data and research-engineering proof.
- **Nocturnal:** public-interest intelligence / evidence-systems proof.
- **Policy Lab:** mechanism/policy-systems proof.

## Support campaign — Economics and runway

Economics is a census, not a shortlist. Every route keeps a compensation/funding state even when the answer is unpublished or unresolved.

Required dimensions: cash amount/range, certainty, duration, tuition/fee support, bonus/equity if material, employment/stacking restrictions, location burden and long-run income path.

Coverage target remains **149/149**.

## Shared infrastructure — build once, reuse everywhere

The highest-ROI work is the work that collapses dozens of route-specific tasks into a common source-of-truth layer:

- **Master identity packet** — canonical biography, education, work/research history, project claims, skills and links.
- **Evidence registry** — every important CV/SOP/resume claim mapped to inspectable proof/revision.
- **Recommender packet** — research summary, CV, deadlines, route groups and suggested emphasis.
- **Route delta sheet** — only the irreducible differences: professor, research question, role-specific evidence, forms and declarations.
- **Decision ledger** — compare actual admissions/offers using funding/salary, certainty, research value, credential conversion, location and option value.

## Operating principle

> **Build shared assets first when one artifact unlocks many routes; spend route-specific effort only on irreducible deltas.**

That is the Main Quest execution model.

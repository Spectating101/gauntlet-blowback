### Phase 3 — live route census and allocation

For every possible route:

1. open the current official source;
2. verify the deadline, eligibility, deliverables, fees, team/adviser/reference requirements, AI-disclosure rules, and account path;
3. compare all plausible lead assets rather than inheriting a stale asset assignment;
4. identify the campaign transformation the route serves;
5. classify the route as `AUTO_PREPARE`, `WAITING_HUMAN`, `HOLD_INTERNAL`, `HOLD_EXTERNAL`, `EXCLUDE`, `EXPIRED`, or `WATCH`;
6. record a verification timestamp and the exact source URL.

Routes requiring unavailable teammates, references, advisers, certifications, mandatory payment, or pre-existing external validation are excluded unless the user explicitly reopens them. A route may still accept an internally validated project when external validation is not an eligibility prerequisite, provided all limitations remain explicit.

### Phase 5 — browser execution

For each `AUTO_PREPARE` route:

1. generate or refresh the FIRE handoff;
2. use the user's existing Chrome session and the designated account;
3. keep no more than five tabs open;
4. inspect the current form adaptively;
5. fill canonical factual fields and route-specific copy;
6. upload designated artifacts;
7. save a draft whenever possible;
8. checkpoint current URL, completed work, unresolved items, and the smallest human action;
9. stop at CAPTCHA, OTP, password creation, payment, terms/privacy consent, authorship/originality declarations, adviser/team commitments, eligibility attestations, or final submission;
10. after the human final action, capture and persist the durable receipt.

### Phase 6 — scheduled operation

Scheduling may begin only after one complete dry run reaches `SAFE_COMPLETE` or `WAITING_HUMAN` without selecting a paused or stale route. Gate Zero passes the full automated suite; the remaining prerequisite is the real-browser dry run.

The scheduled loop should:

1. refresh official-source observations;
2. expire or pause invalid routes;
3. detect asset revision changes;
4. rebuild readiness without silently promoting routes;
5. produce a bounded queue;
6. execute reversible browser preparation sequentially;
7. return one consolidated human-gate queue;
8. ingest receipts and remove completed routes;
9. rerun radar for uncovered campaign objectives.

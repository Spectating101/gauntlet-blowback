## Objective

Run one small, no-cost, externally witnessed evaluation of Nocturnal against an ordinary fact-checking/research workflow.

The pilot is not a request for endorsement and is not a request for TFC to validate marketing claims. It is a workflow comparison designed to produce a falsifiable result.

## Problem being tested

The target problem is not basic web search. It is longitudinal reconstruction.

A public matter may begin with one report, notice, allegation or official action, then accumulate responses, corrections, revised notices, proceedings, closures or later outcomes across different pages and dates. The analyst may therefore have to reconstruct the same history repeatedly and can easily recover the first event without seeing the later state that changes its meaning.

## Nocturnal's proposed solution

Nocturnal maintains one source-linked matter history instead of flattening those records into one summary or leaving them as disconnected search results.

It preserves:

- previous and current documented state;
- source-linked chronology;
- corrections, disputes, superseding records and later outcomes;
- historical `as_of` reconstruction;
- Before ↔ After comparison;
- evidence/source inspection without browser-side truth, guilt, credibility or reputation scoring.

## Interface being evaluated

The completed public product has three reading depths over the same public-safe state:

1. **Now** — readable current journalism: what matters have materially changed and where they stand now.
2. **Read** — a maintained matter history: `What changed?`, chronology, evidence, corrections/disputes, current documented state and historical `as_of` views.
3. **Research** — a read-only investigative workspace: Timeline, Before ↔ After comparison, Subjects, Sources and Evidence inspection.

Canonical product/evaluator references:

- Repository: `https://github.com/Spectating101/nocturnal-oversight`
- Evaluator guide: `https://github.com/Spectating101/nocturnal-oversight/blob/main/EVALUATOR_GUIDE.md`

Do **not** use `edition-nocturnal-preview.vercel.app` in outreach. That hosted preview belongs to the superseded Edition/RC1 line and does not expose the canonical Now → Read → Research path.

## Proposed pilot

- Duration: 1–2 weeks of elapsed time, with roughly 60–90 minutes of partner interaction.
- Case: one closed or bounded evolving public-information case using lawful public sources.
- Preferred first-case classes: public-health guidance revision, environmental/public-safety notice, infrastructure/service incident, product or consumer advisory, or another non-sensitive correction-rich case chosen by the partner.
- Avoid for the first pilot: private-person dossiers, harassment targets, punitive profiling, confidential source material, or a case where the partner cannot safely share workflow notes.
- Data: public sources only unless the partner explicitly supplies a safe, non-sensitive internal workflow artifact.

## Comparison protocol

Use the partner's normal process as baseline. The partner does not need to change tools or reveal confidential methodology.

Measure:

- time to reconstruct the current documented state;
- time to find the supporting source set;
- whether corrections/superseding records are discovered and correctly attached;
- duplicate checking or repeated source-reading avoided;
- analyst error notes / missed-context notes;
- qualitative usefulness: what Nocturnal made easier, what it made harder, and what was irrelevant.

A negative result is acceptable and should be recorded.

## What we do **not** claim

- Nocturnal is not a truth, guilt, reputation or source-credibility scoring system.
- Co-appearance is not evidence of a relationship.
- Source recurrence is not independent corroboration.
- The pilot does not establish newsroom adoption or field effectiveness beyond the exact case.
- TFC participation would not imply endorsement.

## Product that Gauntlet may package

Nocturnal is a longitudinal evidence and public-memory system that preserves how documented matters change over time: source-linked records, identity state, corrections, disputes, later outcomes, and current documented state.

The canonical human-facing product has three depths over the same public-safe backend:

1. **Now** — continuous readable journalism. Newspaper is the visual language, not a daily-reset product constraint.
2. **Read** — sustained factual matter history with governed media, `What changed?`, chronology, corrections/disputes, evidence, and historical `as_of` views.
3. **Research** — read-only investigation with Timeline, Before ↔ After comparison, Subjects graph, Sources trace, and Evidence inspection.

The earlier RC1 / `EDITION` frontend line is superseded and its PR #15 is closed.

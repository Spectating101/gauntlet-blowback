# Portfolio Slot Allocation Audit — 2026-09-01

This is the portfolio-wide control layer above project-specific Gauntlets.

A route may fit several assets. That does **not** mean every fitting asset should be submitted, nor that the first project-specific packet to become ready owns the opportunity.

## Allocation rule

Before committing a consequential application, determine:

```text
opportunity
  ↓
submission unit
  ↓
slot owner + formal/practical cap
  ↓
all plausible portfolio contenders
  ↓
literal fit × current maturity × external evidence × marginal work × payout/upside × opportunity cost
  ↓
LEAD | BUNDLE | PARALLEL | HOLD | KILL
```

The current master already models manuscript mutual exclusion. This audit extends the same discipline to entity, team, person, research-concept, resource and first-grant scarcity.

## InnoServe-specific portfolio result

The current official rules allow a college team to enter at most **two categories**, with at most one entry in each category. The portfolio has far more than two plausible mappings, so the parent program must be optimized globally rather than project-by-project.

Current preliminary ranking, before adviser/team and final track audits:

1. **Hardware Splicer → Industrial AI Innovation** — extremely literal industrial/AI fit; prepared demo material; only structural adviser dependency is known. The official Industrial AI track explicitly rewards innovation, practicality and technical completeness.
2. **Cite / Research Drive → AMD AI Agent** — direct agentic-system fit and an actually operating research/agent stack; requires AMD-resource use at finals, so integration cost must remain bounded.
3. **Policy Lab → Commercial Governance AI or International English** — mature public demo/reproducibility and strong English packaging; potentially excellent, but choose the exact category after checking required data/use-case fit.
4. **Nocturnal → Security Applications** — now highly legible and mature as a product surface, but security is not its primary ontology and Edition still needs a bounded real-data pilot. Do not consume one of two team slots merely because the frontend is impressive.
5. **Citation Engine → Security Technology / AI Tool** — strong kernel, but the submission must demonstrate a concrete security tool rather than generic evidence architecture.

The ranking can change if actual teams are legally distinct, adviser availability differs, or a category-specific requirement gives one project a major advantage. Do not infer unlimited team formation from the two-category rule.

## Resource-layer result

The new student/research-infrastructure radar needs the same allocation discipline:

- **Student entitlements** such as Azure for Students, Heroku student credits, Appwrite Education and YZU software/library access are account-level cost displacement. They should follow real workloads and terms, not be reserved for the most prestigious project.
- **Anthropic Model Hardware Standard preview** is effectively Hardware-Splicer-native.
- **Anthropic AI for Science** should go to a genuine scientific evaluation/benchmark; Hardware Splicer is currently the strongest mapped project, not because it is prestigious but because the scientific/electronics evaluation use is literal.
- **Google Cloud Academic Research Credits** are PI/degree constrained; current radar says the MSc student cannot directly consume that route. If an eligible PI is involved, use the strongest cloud-heavy research workload (Research Drive/digital-asset data infrastructure/finance research) rather than generic hosting.
- **OpenAI Researcher Access** should be allocated to the controlled study with the clearest responsible-AI/research question and actual API budget. It is not a prize for whichever app has the best frontend.

## Bottom line

The portfolio is strongest when projects **specialize** rather than all competing for every broad AI/public-interest route.

- Nocturnal has become a serious lead asset, but should lose InnoServe/SSI-like allocations when another asset is more literal.
- Policy Lab owns finance/policy and is currently ahead on DPG.
- Refinery owns its software-research contribution family.
- Hardware Splicer owns physical/industrial AI.
- Public-Good owns mission/operator civic routes.
- Citation/Research Drive are often the best *evidence infrastructure* inside person-level applications rather than standalone project submissions.

A project-specific `FIRE` recommendation is therefore subordinate to this portfolio allocation layer whenever the opportunity has a scarce slot.

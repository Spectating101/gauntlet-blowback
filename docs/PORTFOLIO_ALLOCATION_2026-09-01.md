# Portfolio Allocation — 2026-09-01

This is the portfolio-side authority for deciding **which asset leads which Gauntlet route**.

It exists because `assets=A|B|C` means only that several assets may be relevant. It does **not** mean all three should be pitched equally, nor that three separate submissions should be created.

## Allocation doctrine

1. **One route, one lead narrative** unless the route hires/funds the person rather than a project.
2. Jobs, RAs, predocs, PhDs, fellowships and residencies use a **person-level evidence bundle**. Projects do not compete for separate applications to the same personal slot.
3. Citation Engine and Gauntlet/Blowback are **support-only**. They strengthen proof and engineering credibility but do not consume standalone project/calendar slots.
4. TWNIC, NLnet, manuscript overlap groups and other scarce mechanisms require a **portfolio bake-off** before submission preparation can cross the allocation gate.
5. Internal technical maturity does not manufacture external validation.
6. No new project, cosmetic AI layer, fake partner, fake adviser or new research thesis is created merely to fit a route.
7. Route-specific work is allowed only when it materially increases selection odds or closes a named evidence gate.

## Assessment

External funding strengthens the case for a doctorate organised around research, finance and programming. The useful comparison is the whole feasible arrangement: scholarship, separately paid research/teaching, grant-funded personal labour, paid projects and actual living costs. None of the future amounts is awarded or contracted yet. No application, message, nomination, consent or commitment was made in this review.

The strongest strategy is to establish dependable doctoral support and a genuinely additional paid research role, then pursue one closely related external project. This can generate research results, useful software and income from overlapping subject matter without inventing commercial experience. It still requires distinct funded deliverables and an honest allocation of time and costs. A project budget is not entirely personal income, and the same assistantship must not be counted twice.

The PhD adds access to supervisors, institutional nominations, collaborators and university-administered research funding. Several independent project grants below can also be pursued without a PhD; they are not exclusive doctoral benefits. Their compatibility with any employment alternative would depend on that employer's contract and workload.

## Practical project choice

Lead with one externally useful, releasable part of the existing work. The most promising new connection is a tool and evaluation for choosing models/tools under a fixed research-workflow budget. Measure task completion/quality, actual API and compute cost, human review time and failure cases against simpler baselines. This connects the allocation research direction with Sentient's stated cost-optimisation interest. It is a proposed experiment, not a completed performance claim or a ready application.

An alternative smaller open/self-hosted research component could suit FUTO if it provides clear user control and works for someone beyond the author. Do not imply existing outside adoption. For university/industry research, use the finance workflow, data reconciliation and model-validation strengths already supported by the employment package. Supervisor agreement, access to people/data and IP/release permissions are unresolved. No private repository is designated for public release here.

Reuse existing CV, research direction, source-backed project descriptions and demonstrations. Add a funder-specific research question, work plan, milestones, permitted-use budget, licensing/IP position and honest funding-overlap statement. Faculty letters and nominations must be requested with authorisation; do not invent commitments. Emergent Ventures and NLnet have their own authorship restrictions: this map does not author application prose for either.

## How grants affect the NT$70K target

Use three separate figures:

1. **Dependable monthly cash:** an actual scholarship plus separately contracted assistantships/teaching. Only include accepted terms and known payment months.
2. **Additional personal grant income:** the approved amount available to compensate the user's work after project costs and institutional treatment. Allocate over the funded period for comparison; preserve actual payment dates for cash flow.
3. **Cost offsets:** credits, lab compute, equipment and reimbursements. These may reduce actual spending, but are not cash wages. Cap any saving at the expense that would otherwise have been paid personally.

Illustration only, all in TWD: a NT$300K project over twelve months that permits NT$180K for the user's labour, NT$60K compute and NT$60K other costs adds NT$15K/month on an annualised basis. Combined with a hypothetical NT$40K base and a genuinely additional NT$20K RA contract, it reaches NT$75K/month without a separate gig. If the same NT$180K is already paying that RA contract, the increment is zero. A grant paying late does not provide this monthly liquidity. The grant amount is invented solely to demonstrate budgeting, not attributed to any funder.

This establishes a feasible mechanism, not an expected award. No success rates, independent-win assumptions or aggregate annual grant income are estimated. Do not add several alternative fellowships or multiple grants paying for the same work. Allocate proposed hours as well as cash: overlapping research subjects do not make contractual work hours disappear.

## Sequence

- **Before admission:** inspect the Sentient/FUTO fit, retain EV as applicant-authored, and prepare a factual evidence/budget inventory. Preserve prior application states. No new submission authority follows from this research request.
- **With admission and supervisor:** obtain the complete scholarship/RA breakdown, ask about a paid research/industry work package, and confirm NSTC-quota access and the next actual cycle. Check university outside-funding/IP rules and the work classification of proposed gigs.
- **After enrolment/results:** target one deliverable-based project and suitable institutional fellowships. Google requires coursework progress; NVIDIA normally requires a completed doctoral year. Monitor published future calls rather than assuming annual dates repeat.

The best doctorate for this strategy has a supervisor who supports the research, has real project access and can explain what paid work is additional. A prestigious programme without those conditions does not establish the earning arrangement. A well-matched paid research job may also support some independent grants, so compare actual packages when available.

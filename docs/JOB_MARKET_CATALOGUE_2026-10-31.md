## How to read this

- **104 detail** means the signed-in browser displayed the individual job description on 31 October 2026; a visible job is not proof the employer will accept an application in March 2027.
- **Cake detail** means an indexed individual Cake posting was retrieved on 31 October; the signed-in browser encountered a security-verification page during this search. Do not infer that the application form was verified or the position is currently open just because the detail was visible.
- Salary is the employer's advertised gross figure, not an offer, a prediction or a guaranteed bonus. Annual figures divided by 12 are annualized monthly equivalents; actual salary months and bonus guarantees may differ. “Negotiable; NT$40K+” on 104 is a disclosure threshold, not an offer or an estimate.
- **First wave** = persuasive profile overlap with no clear disqualifier, subject to timing and rights. **Adjacent** = useful work that captures part of the blend. **Stretch** = materially higher technical, tenure, language, industry or trading requirements; not an automatic rejection. These are judgments, not probability estimates.

Synthetic project results are not live investment results or deployed client outcomes. Mandarin B1/B2 is an aspiration pending examination, not an earned credential. Holds a student work permit; post-graduation work arrangements are not confirmed. March 2027 preferred, April negotiable; employer acceptance is unconfirmed. Neili by public transport, relocation conditional on offer and costs.

## Interpretation and preparation

1. **A real intersection exists.** TAROBO 6vt43, Apex research, KGI's quant-data role and Cathay's AI/data analyst all connect financial questions with data or software. Kapito is a practical technical alternative; it does not have to combine all three interests.
2. **Pay varies by role and access conditions.** Several observed roles advertise 800K–1.2M/year or more, but some high-end postings also require live trading, a specialised degree, advanced modelling or professional Mandarin. This sample is curated, not a market-wide distribution. Do not predict an individual offer from a range.
3. **Three evidence packs can be reused:** (a) finance/data: master's, RA API work, working paper and careful data interpretation; (b) quant tooling: Sharpe chronology, costs and missing-data synthetic checks with limitations; (c) research software: Cite-Refinery revision/API validation and AI-assisted workflow evidence. Explain individual contribution and review shareability; no unsupported deployment, financial performance, live trading or model-training claims.
4. **Before choosing an application**, verify exact role open, graduation/earliest start, degree acceptance, language in actual meetings, work-rights pathway, base vs guaranteed annual pay, bonus assumptions, office, attendance and commute. User is unwilling to force an employer's deadline. Estimate actual public-transport door-to-door time and rents only once office and schedule are known; no generic district-to-Neili time is assumed.
5. **Source overlap:** Apex research and BA share one employer/team; TAROBO's two posts share an employer; Cathay posts are separate jobs but the same organization. One employer may post on both sites. Deduplicate by role/employer before any outreach. A role labelled “easy apply” is not an application and is never a reason to submit automatically.

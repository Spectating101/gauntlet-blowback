## Plain product description

Refinery helps developers inspect existing software before rebuilding or relying on it. It indexes repositories, separates provisional discovery from reviewed capabilities, and ties evidence to a specific implementation. Its reuse evaluation records the evidence, gaps and execution-mapping state behind a decision. A separate audited market-research workflow describes products and capabilities without treating recurrence or popularity as demand.

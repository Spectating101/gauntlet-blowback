# Portfolio Campaign Graph — 2026-09-16

## Decision

The Gauntlet calendar is an execution surface, not the portfolio plan. The operating hierarchy is:

```text
desired outcome
    -> campaign
        -> asset transformation and decisive gate
            -> opportunity serving a declared function
                -> packet and portal work
                    -> external outcome and evidence feedback
```

This graph is deliberately open-world. It maps the currently evidenced campaign families without claiming that they are the complete portfolio. New assets, papers, objectives, and opportunity families enter as `NEEDS_CAMPAIGN_ASSIGNMENT`; they remain researchable but cannot enter automatic execution until assigned.

The machine-readable authority is `data/portfolio-campaign-graph-2026-09-16.json`.

Academic and system outcomes may strengthen Main Quest packets, but they do not exist merely as resume decoration. Publications, external scholarly verdicts, research resources, standards review, product evidence, and user/operator evidence remain legitimate campaign outcomes of their own.

Funded fellowships, residencies, predocs, labs, and visiting-research appointments are kept distinct from ordinary employment because their duration, authority, personal cash, restricted support, and career option value differ. Likewise, resource acquisition and paid market conversion are explicit portfolio campaigns rather than miscellaneous route statuses.

## Route-function vocabulary

Every attached opportunity must say what it does for the campaign:

- `FUND_EXTEND`
- `SPECIALIST_VALIDATE`
- `CROSS_DOMAIN_STRESS_TEST`
- `PUBLISH`
- `DISSEMINATE`
- `RESOURCE_ACQUIRE`
- `STANDARD_REVIEW`
- `PILOT_VALIDATE`
- `CAPABILITY_DEMONSTRATE`
- `MARKET_TEST`
- `CAREER_CONVERT`

An opportunity that cannot explain its function remains a radar lead, not campaign work.

## Independent readiness axes

A route cannot become executable merely because one label says `FIRE` or because a form is reachable. It must separately clear:

1. `asset_readiness` — the project or paper supports the proposal honestly;
2. `campaign_usefulness` — the route advances the current transformation;
3. `packet_readiness` — copy and designated artifacts exist and pass QA;
4. `portal_readiness` — the current form, account, deadline and eligibility are verified;
5. `economics_acceptability` — fees, travel, time and restrictions are known and acceptable;
6. `submission_authority` — protected personal/legal/final actions are authorized.

# Refinery research-venue route card — MSR / SANER family

## Contribution boundary

For software-engineering research venues, **Refinery is the contribution**. Nocturnal and Edition are downstream consumers/demonstrators and should not hijack the paper.

## Strongest empirical material

1. exact Capability / Implementation / Claim identity;
2. `MEV0-001` evidence-laundering defect and exact-subject repair;
3. negative generic build-vs-reuse control: RFC 3986 reuse 42/42 vs clean-room 42/42 → no reuse advantage;
4. positive bounded curated-state control: licensed PSL reuse 78/78 vs code-only rebuild 39/78 → +50 percentage points;
5. independent exact-subject uv provenance verification;
6. deterministic reviewer/closure artifacts and public reviewer deployment;
7. negative results preserved rather than converted into a success story.

## MSR 2027 Technical Papers

Current master retains `msr-2027-technical-refinery`. Rehydrate the exact CFP and deadline before FIRE.

Best paper question: when implementation becomes cheap, what empirical evidence distinguishes reconstructable generic behavior from scarce accumulated state, and how should evidence attach to exact realizations without laundering across siblings?

Edition can appear near the end as a consequential consumer of the evidence model, not as “the AI news product paper.”

## MSR 2027 Data & Tool Showcase

Current master retains `msr-2027-data-tool-refinery`.

This route may be cleaner if the artifact/reproducibility requirements fit the frozen Commons, evidence objects, reviewer surface and paired empirical controls better than a full technical-paper contribution.

## SANER

Keep separate lane choices:

- Research paper if the empirical question is primary;
- Tool demo if the artifact/evidence workflow is primary;
- Agentic AI4SE only if agent behavior becomes a genuine measured object. A presentation layer using AI does not satisfy that by itself.

## Non-claims

Do not claim universal reuse superiority, PMF, or that public availability implies rights/value. The paired positive/negative result is strong precisely because it can return both outcomes.

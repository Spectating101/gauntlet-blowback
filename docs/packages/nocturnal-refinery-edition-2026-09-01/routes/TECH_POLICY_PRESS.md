## Why Nocturnal/Edition helps

This is one of the routes where the new public surface materially improves the application. A reviewer can see an actual information-integrity product rather than infer it from backend documentation:

- changing public state;
- source/perspective landscape;
- correction and supersession;
- regional/country projection;
- clear separation of documented evidence from derived interpretation.

Policy Lab/Citation Engine can support the broader governance/evidence story, but Nocturnal should remain the concrete demonstration.

## External-validation gaps that remain real

The following are **not packaging debt** and must not be hidden by stronger visuals:

1. no validated real-user adoption or repeat use;
2. no measured time-to-understanding advantage on a real corpus yet;
3. no measured stewardship minutes per 1,000 real records/modules;
4. no broad source-rights/legal clearance for unrestricted real-person publication;
5. no globally distributed high-frequency production ingestion benchmark;
6. no evidence that an external newsroom, institution or research group has adopted the stack;
7. Refinery has no general external-value / product-market-fit proof;
8. GitHub-hosted Actions provisioning remains unhealthy for some private exact-head runs.

Those gaps are why the package ends in a pilot/evaluation ask rather than a universal production claim.

## What changed because Edition exists

Edition changes **conversion quality**, not truth conditions:

- cold reviewers can understand the longitudinal-state thesis without reading backend docs first;
- the stack now demonstrates an end-to-end product path from observation abundance to finite human comprehension;
- public-interest grants can see the proposed deployment surface;
- lab/PhD applications can show research-engineering breadth with one coherent artifact;
- Refinery submissions gain a concrete downstream consumer without needing to pretend Edition is the research contribution;
- pilot asks become specific and testable.

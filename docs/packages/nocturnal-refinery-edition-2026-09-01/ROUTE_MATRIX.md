## Important venue-specific findings

### TWNIC

The official 2026 announcement says each legally established applying unit may submit **one proposal**. This is a genuine scarce portfolio/institution slot, not merely a Nocturnal gate. Nocturnal is currently the strongest natural match to TWNIC's internet-policy/internet-governance remit, but that judgement must be compared against the whole portfolio and the host's own pipeline before submission.

### NLnet

The master already contains both a **Policy Lab Restack** route and a **Nocturnal Restack** route. NLnet permits multiple proposals, but its Restack FAQ tells first-time applicants to focus on making one project successful and says multiple eligible proposals may ultimately need to be chosen between or plausibly combined. Treat all first-grant NLnet candidates as an internal portfolio tournament before submission.

### Digital Public Goods

Policy Lab and Nocturnal are different digital solutions. The current DPG process evaluates each application/solution against the DPG Standard; no one-solution-per-applicant cap was found in the current public FAQ. This is a priority problem, not an exclusivity problem. Policy Lab is presently the more execution-ready route in the Gauntlet master.

### Personal applications

Sinica labs, Tech Policy Press, PhDs and similar opportunities hire/support the **person**. The project portfolio supplies evidence. Do not manufacture separate applications for Nocturnal, Cite and Research Drive to the same position. Compile one strongest bundle.

# Core Narrative

## 50-word form

Refinery reduces noisy heterogeneous reporting into structured observations and evidence roles. Nocturnal preserves the documented state of matters across time, including provenance, ambiguity, corrections and supersession. Edition turns that persistent state into finite Global, regional and country briefings with inspectable timelines, sources and impact views. Presentation AI never becomes canonical evidence.

## 150-word form

Modern news systems are very good at publishing what happened now and much worse at preserving what happened **next**. A reader returning to a long-running story often has to reconstruct the current state from many articles, later corrections and conflicting claims.

The stack separates that problem into three layers. **Refinery** reduces heterogeneous reporting into normalized observations, clusters and source-role evidence without treating clustering as truth. **Nocturnal 1.2** maintains longitudinal matter history: evidence grades, source trails, identity ambiguity, explicit corrections/disputes, publication-risk controls and the current documented state. **Edition RC1** is the reader-facing projection: a finite editorial edition with Global / Region / Country views and interactive Brief / Timeline / Sources / Impact dossiers.

The architecture is deliberately authority-bounded. Presentation intelligence can rank, summarize, project impact and choose a visual treatment, but cannot silently rewrite Nocturnal’s evidence, correction history or identity state. The next milestone is external validation on a bounded real corpus, not another feature sprint.

## Full submission form

The project addresses a mismatch between the volume of public reporting and the way people actually need to understand evolving events. News arrives as an abundance of separate pages, syndications, updates and corrections. Search can retrieve those pages, but a reader or researcher still has to decide which items describe the same development, which claims are independent, what changed, which earlier statements are no longer current, and what remains unresolved.

The system is built as three authority-separated layers.

**Refinery** handles abundance. It normalizes heterogeneous observations, preserves exact-subject evidence, distinguishes source/evidence roles, clusters related developments and keeps negative or unclustered results rather than laundering them into certainty.

**Nocturnal 1.2** handles memory. It is a frozen longitudinal public-memory engine with evidence grades, strict hash/chain verification, ambiguity-aware identity review, matter histories, citation bundles, append-only correction/dispute links, fail-closed publication controls, public/intake/operator trust boundaries, deterministic public snapshots and reproducible 5k/50k scale gates.

**Edition RC1** handles comprehension. It renders the shared matter state as finite Global, regional and country editions rather than an infinite feed. A story opens into an interactive dossier: current documented state, Before → Now, chronology, source landscape and impact projection. Presentation and media contracts are versioned and rights-aware. Derived impact edges must declare whether they are observed, modelled or an analytical hypothesis.

The system does not claim to replace reporting or discover facts that never enter the evidence ecosystem. It does not claim unrestricted truth detection, real-world adoption, global production readiness or cleared rights for arbitrary source media. Its current defensible state is product-complete and technically pilot-ready, with external validation still required.

## One-line positioning by venue

- Public-interest: **A living public record that keeps corrections and later outcomes attached to the history they change.**
- Research software: **An evidence-bounded pipeline from heterogeneous observations to persistent state and inspectable presentation.**
- Research labor / PhD: **A demonstrated end-to-end research system spanning data acquisition, evidence semantics, longitudinal state and usable interactive delivery.**

# Audience Projections

One evidence family, three legitimate projections. Never rewrite facts to make a venue fit.

## PUBLIC_INTEREST

### 50-word pitch

A longitudinal public-memory system for evolving public-interest matters. Nocturnal preserves source trails, identity ambiguity, corrections and later outcomes; Edition turns that state into finite, inspectable briefings instead of an endless article feed. Refinery reduces reporting abundance upstream. The system is technically pilot-ready but still requires a bounded responsible real-world evaluation.

### Lead with

- current documented state and `Before → Now`;
- correction/dispute preservation;
- provenance and source-role visibility;
- public/intake/operator authority separation;
- finite Global / Region / Country editions;
- rights-aware media and fail-closed publication;
- concrete pilot metrics.

### De-emphasize

- software ontology terminology unless asked;
- generic AI novelty claims;
- “replacement for journalism” framing.

### Best routes

OTF ICRP, TWNIC, Tech Policy Press, DPG, later APNIC/Asia House, partner-led Pulitzer/FIJ.

## RESEARCH_SOFTWARE

### 50-word pitch

An evidence-bounded research/software stack for deciding what information or capability actually exists, which evidence belongs to which exact subject, what changed over time, and how that state can be reproduced and inspected. Refinery provides the empirical build-vs-reuse/evidence-identity contribution; Nocturnal and Edition demonstrate a consequential downstream consumer.

### Lead with

- Refinery Capability / Implementation / Claim identity;
- `MEV0-001` evidence-laundering defect and repair;
- negative RFC 3986 reuse control;
- positive licensed PSL +50pp bounded result;
- deterministic registries/reviewer bundle;
- exact-subject provenance;
- Nocturnal/Edition only as downstream application evidence.

### De-emphasize

- news/publication aesthetics;
- policy advocacy;
- generic “AI newsroom” terminology.

### Best routes

MSR Technical, MSR Data & Tool, SANER Research/Tool Demo, NLnet where call fit is real.

## RESEARCH_LAB_PHD

### 50-word pitch

A completed research-engineering stack demonstrating the full path from heterogeneous data acquisition and evidence normalization to persistent longitudinal state, provenance-aware correction handling and an interactive reader interface. The strongest value for a lab is not the product brand; it is evidence that the applicant can formulate invariants, implement systems, pressure-test them and stop at defensible empirical boundaries.

### Lead with

- research question → invariant → implementation → adverse test → evidence boundary;
- large-data and source-normalization experience;
- identity ambiguity and correction semantics;
- 5k/50k release benchmarks;
- Refinery’s positive and negative controls;
- Edition as evidence of product/UX translation.

### De-emphasize

- startup/brand language;
- claims of newsroom expertise;
- “finished product means no research left.” The next research question is external validation and measurement.

### Best routes

Academia Sinica Ku Lab, Der-Nian Yang Lab, VU Social Data Science after exact proposal-fit review, other information-integrity/social-data labs discovered by Gauntlet.

## Shared closing ask

Do not end with “please admire the project.” End with a bounded next transaction:

- fund a defined open-infrastructure tranche;
- host a bounded pilot;
- evaluate the system on a real corpus;
- hire the applicant to extend/test the research question;
- review a specific empirical contribution.
